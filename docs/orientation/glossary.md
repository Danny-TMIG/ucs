# Glossary

- Cognitive identity: durable continuity binding.
- Principal: the user or workload Govern authenticates.
- Capability: a bounded action Compute can realize.
- GovernedOutcome: Govern's sealed result.
- ExecutionOrder: the sole Govern-to-Compute command.
- EvidenceEnvelope: the cross-layer evidence object.
- Open decision: unresolved choice in decisions/open/.
- Research gate: a -MATH-* maturity level or research ID.
